Selected Research

Research

Multimodal Gaussian Process Fusion

Built a reproducible pipeline benchmarking a multimodal ensemble Gaussian process — per-modality distributions fused by moment matching — against neural-network and transformer baselines on an 8-dataset benchmark, reaching 0.72 test R² on Melbourne and matching a 5-model deep ensemble on accuracy while beating it on calibration (NLPD 0.4330 vs 0.4447). A 9,703-configuration ablation (9 embedders × 3 GP scaling methods × 5 ensemble modes) showed spectrally normalized networks between encoders and GP heads raised R² from 0.629 to 0.662.

Research with K. Polyzos, Schmidt AI in Science (UCSD) · manuscript in preparation

Hybrid RAG over Technical Docs

Co-developed a hybrid RAG pipeline — hierarchical chunking, bge-m3 dense + BM25 sparse retrieval fused by reciprocal-rank fusion, then a bge cross-encoder reranker — reaching F1@5 0.853 / Precision@5 0.889. Designed a HyDE query stage (an LLM drafts a hypothetical answer whose embedding drives retrieval) with parent-chunk expansion, raising F1 from 0.6 to 0.85.

Team project (2 people) · Read the paper

FretBoardCrazies – Guitar Fretboard Trainer (Summer 2026)

  • Native SwiftUI app for iOS and macOS that prompts a note and verifies it from live microphone input, detecting pitch through AudioKit's PitchTap.
  • Modular by design: generalized a guitar-only app to bass, banjo, and ukulele by injecting an Instrument abstraction through the drill, progress and preset layers over five refactors — deleting hardcoded string ranges instead of branching on type.
  • Built spec-first alongside Claude Code: 17 design specs and 16 implementation plans committed before the features they describe, with 8 protocol seams and 254 Swift Testing cases keeping agent-written changes verifiable.
  • A music-theory engine suggests the next chord from the current chord and key — 23 chord qualities, 15 harmonic rules, gated behind a 4-tier progression.
